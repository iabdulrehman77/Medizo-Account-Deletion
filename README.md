# Medizo — Account Deletion

## Request to Delete Your Medizo Account

If you have a Medizo account and would like to delete your account and associated data, you can request deletion by emailing us.

### How to request account deletion

1. Send an email to **itsrehmn@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line **Medizo Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include the email address associated with your Medizo account in your request.
4. We may contact you to verify your request before processing it.

### Data that will be deleted

Once your deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete your account-related personal data and associated account information, except for information we are legally required or permitted to retain.

### Data that may be retained

Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes. Any retained information will be kept only for the applicable required retention period and handled in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable laws.

### Processing time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within **30 days**. If additional time is required, we will notify you.
